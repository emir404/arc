# Phase 1 notes: IB guide + 29/30 model essay (read in full 2026-09-18)

## IB format guide (uploaded, pp.12-16 of a school EE handbook, quoting IB EE Guide pp.82-87)
- Six required elements: title page, contents page, introduction, body, conclusion, references & bibliography.
- Title page: title (summative statement, NOT phrased as a question), research question, subject, word count. NO candidate/school name anywhere.
- Formatting: Arial 12, double spacing, numbered pages; doc/docx/pdf/rtf; ~10MB max.
- Word limit: 4,000 words of narrative; examiners stop reading beyond it; exceeding compromises all criteria.
- Counted: introduction, body, conclusion, quotations, footnotes/endnotes that are not references.
- NOT counted: contents page, maps/charts/diagrams/annotated illustrations, TABLES, equations/formulas/calculations, citations/references (parenthetical, numbered, footnotes or endnotes), bibliography, RPPF.
- Appendices are not read; anything important must be in the body. Contents page required; index not required.
- Introduction: focus, scope, indication of sources to be used, insight into line of argument.
- Body: reasoned argument; clear what evidence was found, where/how, and how it supports the argument; sub-headings acceptable; follow subject conventions.
- Conclusion: what has been achieved, limitations, unresolved questions; must relate to the RQ.
- Referencing: consistent style from the start (IB "Effective citing and referencing"). Harvard preferred by user.

## Model essay (Economics EE, "To what extent are pricing decisions in coffee shops in Sevenoaks interdependent?", 29/30, grade A; marked on criteria for first exams May 2027: A Framework, B Knowledge & understanding, C Analysis & line of argument, D Discussion & evaluation, E Reflection)
Structure: Title + word count; Contents; Introduction; Research method; Theoretical hypothesis (Demand / Costs / Interdependence) with a mini "Conclusion" stating predictions; Research findings (Data on quantity; Data on prices; Calculating interdependence via price range; via an equation); Conclusion; Bibliography. Numbered footnotes for every claim, incl. definitions.
Lessons for depth/structure:
1. Intro opens with a cited contextual statistic, defines market structures precisely with cited definitions, states RQ verbatim, explains significance (to consumers/producers, personal link), and previews the method ("basic method ... then use an equation").
2. Research method section evaluates its own sources: primary data (manager interviews) with explicit bias (managers may inflate quantities), standardisation of questions, why interviews > questionnaires, acknowledges secondary sources may be false and how selection mitigates; explains and justifies the choice of the analytical model (Berg & Kim 1994) and why it fits.
3. Theory section derives a HYPOTHESIS per factor before looking at data ("interdependence will be emphasised", "costs are not significant"), each grounded in cited theory and a contextual number (£3.33 cappuccino vs £1 cost).
4. Findings: original calculations (market shares, HHI = 3298, price range 0.34 = 14% of mean, conjectural variation matrix) with tables; each calculation is followed by interpretation AND an explanation of why the result deviates from the hypothesis (economies of scale, artisanal differentiation).
5. Adapts an academic equation to its context, states every modification and assumption explicitly (Fk = 1, wk = 1, swapping price/quantity), and validates the method with three pieces of evidence that it "works", then acknowledges flaws (CV is expectation-based; errors cancel because present in all data).
6. Conclusion answers RQ directly in the first sentence, restates hypothesis vs findings, admits surprise, explains the mechanism, and ends with unresolved questions.
Examiner comments: A 6/6 (RQ clear, process effective, primary + secondary complementary); B 6/6 (skilful application of theory); C 5/6 (strengths/weaknesses of research addressed "but not in sufficient depth to reach the top band"); D full marks (evaluate and synthesise, balanced discussion of importance of the different points); E 4/4.
=> Implication for our essay: the one lost mark was on depth of methodological self-criticism within the line of argument. So: integrate source/methodology limitations INSIDE the analysis, not only in a methodology section; keep calculations transparent with source values; hypothesis stated before evidence; conclusion first sentence answers "to what extent".
