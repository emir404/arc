## 8. Main definitional conflicts between providers (evidence-based)

1. **Same year, three totals.** 2023: startups.watch $722M/325 vs KPMG $497M/297 vs StartupCentrum $901M/347. 2024: $1.1B/469 vs $2.6B/331 vs $1.41B/577. 2025: $589M/306 vs $1.4B/360 vs $622M/359. H1 2026: $172M/87 vs ≈$1.12B/82 (KPMG Q1+Q2, derived) vs $178M/101.
2. **Acquisitions in or out.** KPMG's headline includes M&A of startups (Hepsiburada, Trendyol Go, Loom, Getir units); startups.watch and StartupCentrum headlines do not appear to. This alone explains most of the 2024–2026 gaps.
3. **Deal-count denominators.** StartupCentrum counts BiGG grants and crowdfunding campaigns as rounds (up to ~40% of H1 2026 rounds); startups.watch reports crowdfunding separately; KPMG counts the fewest deals (announced VC/angel/M&A transactions).
4. **Mega-round treatment.** Startups.watch includes Getir's $500M (2023) in its total; KPMG's 2023 total is smaller than that single round, implying exclusion (reason NOT VERIFIED — possibly domicile of the issuing entity or instrument type).
5. **Restatements.** KPMG's 2022 figure appears as $1.5B/239 in the 2022 review and $1.84B/277 in the 2023 review.
6. **Geography.** Startups.watch separates diaspora ("Turkish-founded abroad": $1.1B/41 deals in 2025); Dealroom uses HQ; Invest in Türkiye's unicorn list includes exited/listed companies; Insider is described as "situated in Singapore" by one outlet while listed as HQ Istanbul by Dealroom and as a Turcorn by the Ministry.
7. **"Fastest-growing" claims are valuation-based.** Dealroom/DTO (9x in ecosystem *value* 2019–2024; "fastest-growing in EMEA"), Dealroom GTEI 2025 (Istanbul #2 Rising Star; enterprise-value and unicorn growth adjusted for GDP per capita), Startup Genome GSER 2025 (Istanbul #3 emerging; "423% funding surge in 2024" which relies on the Hepsiburada/Insider/Getir transactions). None of these measure breadth of early-stage funding, which fell in 2025–H1 2026 on every provider's numbers.
8. **Unicorn counts 5–8.** Invest in Türkiye: six (Trendyol, Getir, Peak, Dream Games, Insider, Hepsiburada); Dealroom/DTO and Bazaar Times: eight (adding Papara, HubX — dates/mechanisms single-source); startups.watch adds three *diaspora* unicorns in 2025 (Airalo, Periodic Labs, Fal); Loom Games crossed $1B in 2026 via a 50% sale.
