## 0. Verification method and limitations (READ FIRST)

1. **Page fetches were blocked.** The organisation's network egress policy returned 403 on CONNECT for every non-GitHub host tried (tcmb.gov.tr, data.tuik.gov.tr, news.crunchbase.com, tradingeconomics.com, reuters.com, bloomberg.com, worldbank.org, imf.org, dealroom.co, wikipedia.org, web.archive.org, aa.com.tr, etc.). No PDF could be downloaded, so no "page/table number" can be given for any PDF in this stream.
2. **Verification was therefore by search-result excerpts.** Figures below come from (a) titles/URLs of search results, which are literal excerpts of the source page, and (b) the search tool's synthesis of page content ("summary-level"). Following the coordinator's rule, a summary sentence is only counted as evidence where a result URL is recorded next to it, and it is labelled as summary-level rather than title-level.
3. **The session's WebSearch budget (200 calls, shared across agents) was exhausted after this stream's 16th query.** Topics 3 (VC statements on FX/cost arbitrage), 5 (most regulation items), 6 (flipping), 7 (talent), 8 (penetration/e-commerce), 9 (ratings/earthquake) and 10 (attribution) could NOT be searched. Where the essay needs those items, this file gives (i) what the assistant recalls, explicitly marked **NOT VERIFIED**, and (ii) the exact primary URL/query to run once search capacity is restored. Do not cite a NOT VERIFIED item in the essay without checking it.
4. **GitHub public repositories were the one reachable host.** GitHub code search returned fragments of public CSV/Markdown files that cite TCMB XML files and TÜİK bulletin IDs. These are third-party compilations, so they are labelled "third-party mirror citing primary source" and never counted as primary evidence, but they corroborate several figures.

### 3b. Computed depreciation (from the table; computations are the assistant's)

- End-2022 → end-2025 (both `[mirror]`, TCMB buying): 18.6983 → 42.8457 = lira lost 56.4% of its USD value in three years; USD/TRY +129%.
- Yahoo end-2020 → end-2025: 7.3737 → 42.9520 = lira lost 82.8% of USD value in five years.
- Nominal depreciation vs. inflation: 2023: USD/TRY +57.4% vs Dec-CPI 64.77%; 2024: +19.8% vs 44.38%; 2025: +21.4% vs 30.89%. In 2024 and 2025 the lira depreciated much less than inflation, i.e., **real appreciation** — which raises Turkish startups' USD-equivalent wage and rent costs and erodes the "cost arbitrage" that existed in 2021–2023. This is an inference; cite the underlying figures, not the inference, unless the essay labels it as the author's calculation.
