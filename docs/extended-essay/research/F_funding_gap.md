## 10. Conflicts and definitional cautions (for the essay's evaluation section)

1. **7 % vs 13 % conversion** — both attributed to Startups.watch comparisons with UK/FR/DE (~50 %); most likely 2025 vs 2024 vintages of the same metric, but NOT VERIFIED. The metric's definition (cohort-based vs same-year ratio) is unknown; the 2025 ratio 22/284 ≈ 7.7 % hints at a same-year ratio.
2. **Provider totals disagree** (FY2025: $589M vs $1.4B vs ~$700M vs $416M) because of scope (VC-only vs incl. M&A; Dealroom's HQ rules). Always name the provider and scope.
3. **Foreign share of value is inflated by acquisitions** in KPMG-212 figures; the foreign share of *equity rounds by stage* remains unknown.
4. **"No later-stage deals in 2025"** partly reflects **statistical migration** (post-Series A flips) — the same fact supports both a "funding gap" reading and a "successful startups leave the sample" reading; the essay should present both.
5. **Seed counts are inflated by TÜBİTAK BiGG** grants (≈228 of 247 seed deals in 9M 2024), which mechanically depresses any seed-to-Series-A ratio relative to countries where "seed" means a priced VC round.
6. **Global benchmark comparability**: Carta's 15–31 % (24-month US cohorts) is not comparable with Startups.watch's ~50 % for UK/FR/DE without knowing the latter's method.
