# Research log: VC concentration, scalability and survival of early-stage startups in Türkiye (2020–2026)

Compiled 18 September 2026 for the IB Business Management Extended Essay:
"To what extent does the increasing concentration of venture capital funding in Türkiye influence the scalability and survival rates of early-stage startups?"

## How to read this log (verification status)

- Seven research streams were run (ecosystem data, concentration, new funds, academic literature, macro/alternative explanations, funding gap, firm cases).
- Direct page and PDF fetches were blocked by the network egress policy for every research domain (data providers, journals, government, press). Only web search worked, and the session's search budget (200 queries) was exhausted before all planned cross-checks ran.
- Every figure below therefore rests on search-result titles and the search tool's extracted sentences, not on the source page itself. Labels:
  - **[2+]** the same figure was returned by two or more results from different outlets (strongest available);
  - **[1]** single result or several results tracing to one article;
  - **[S]** appeared only in the search tool's summary sentence with a plausible URL (weakest);
  - **[NV]** not verified: attribution ambiguous, conflicting, or not searched.
- **No PDF page or table numbers could be recorded.** Before any figure is cited in the essay, the listed URL must be opened and the number, date and page confirmed. The most important primary documents are listed in Section 13.
- "Computed" means the ratio is the author's arithmetic on inputs from one provider and one edition; ratios are never computed across providers.

## 4. Stage structure (deal count vs value)

- KPMG-212 2021: seed >220 deals = >85% of deal count; early-VC volume >$400M [1].
- Startups.watch Q1 2024: 158 seed deals (2nd in Europe after UK's 247); 112 pre-seed (1st in Europe and MENA); $111M / 161 rounds [1].
- Startups.watch 9M 2024: 247 seed of 388 deals (64%); ≈228 BiGG-backed (59%) [1].
- KPMG-212 2025: seed 269 of 360 deals (74.7%) [2+]; Q3 2025: seed 8.1% of value despite the most deals; early-stage $155.5M = two-thirds of quarterly value [1].
- Startups.watch 2025: 284 seed / 22 early / 0 later [1]; H1 2025: "difficulties in transitioning from seed stage to Series A and B continued" [1]; 2024 review: "significantly weaker performance compared to other countries beyond the seed stage" [1].
- Median round sizes: StartupCentrum 2025 median $600K [1]; Startup Genome Istanbul median seed $487K, Series A $2M [1]. Capital per startup at start ≈$22K in Türkiye vs $373K UK, $1M France, $280K Germany (Startups.watch via press) [S].

## 12. Definitional conflicts and cautions to state in the essay

1. Three totals per year (2024: $1.1B / $2.6B / $1.41B; 2025: $589M / $1.4B / $622M) because KPMG-212 includes acquisitions and StartupCentrum counts grants and crowdfunding.
2. Deal-count denominators differ by up to 74% (2024: 331 vs 469 vs 577).
3. KPMG-212 restates prior years (2022: $1.5B/239 vs $1.84B/277) and its 2023 total ($497M) is below Startups.watch's single Getir round.
4. Startups.watch's "conversion rate" may be an annual early-stage/seed ratio, not a cohort graduation rate; not comparable with Carta cohorts without checking the definition.
5. Seed counts are inflated by TÜBİTAK BiGG grants (≈228 of 247 seed deals in 9M 2024), mechanically depressing conversion ratios.
6. "No later-stage rounds" partly reflects statistical migration after HQ flips (deals booked abroad), not only failure.
7. "Fastest-growing" claims are valuation-stock metrics (ecosystem value, GTEI, GSER), dominated by a few large companies.
8. Unicorn counts range from 5 to 8 depending on whether exited, listed, foreign-HQ and diaspora companies are included; Insider's HQ is described differently by different sources.
